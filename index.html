About the Artist

Three generations at the bench.

Tom learned silverwork from his grandfather on the Navajo Nation, spending long afternoons watching stamps meet silver before he ever held a hammer himself. Every piece that leaves his bench still follows that same rhythm — heat, hammer, stamp, stone.

Working primarily in sterling silver, sand-cast copper, and natural turquoise, Tom builds pieces meant to be worn daily and passed down — not locked in a drawer. Commissions and custom orders are welcome.
